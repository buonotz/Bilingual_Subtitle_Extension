# Required store assets

These assets still require product screenshots or an approved visual design and
are intentionally not fabricated by the packaging script.

## Extension icons

Add transparent PNG files:

- `icons/icon16.png` — 16×16
- `icons/icon32.png` — 32×32
- `icons/icon48.png` — 48×48
- `icons/icon128.png` — 128×128

For the 128×128 store icon, Chrome recommends a 96×96 visual centered with
16 pixels of transparent padding on each side.

## Screenshots

Provide 1–5 screenshots at 1280×800 (preferred) or 640×400:

1. Netflix with two different official subtitle languages
2. Max with two different official subtitle languages
3. Official language discovery and the extension language list
4. Fullscreen bilingual subtitle display

Crop or blur profile names, email addresses, browser bookmarks, unrelated tabs,
notifications, and operating-system account details.

## Promotional images

- Small promotional tile: 440×280 PNG or JPEG
- Optional marquee image: 1400×560 PNG or JPEG

Do not imply endorsement by Netflix or Max and do not use their logos as the
extension's primary brand.
